# 7 · Robot Learning & Adaptive Systems

National Robotarium · Delivery Board · Horizon Europe 2026–2027

Area 7 of 16 in the opportunity pipeline · Detail drawn from the draft Horizon Europe Work Programme 2026-2027, part 14 Horizontal Activities

This document covers area 7 of the National Robotarium's 16 Horizon Europe 2026-2027 opportunity areas. There is no Cluster 4 topic in Horizontal Activities; the learning and adaptation content sits inside the automated discovery and frontier AI topics.

## Work Programme call and topic

The deck places this under Cluster 4 Digital with a Q3 2026 onward window and a value of 5-12 million euro. Horizontal Activities contains no Cluster 4 topic. The horizontal instrument with real robot-learning content is HORIZON-RAISE-2027-01-01, Automated Scientific Discovery, which requires autonomous or semi-autonomous adaptive AI systems including agentic AI that connect with laboratory instruments and robotics and can autonomously plan, act, learn and adapt within a scientifically valid safe pipeline.

## Indicative value

The deck values this area at 5-12 million euro. The nearest horizontal match is the automated discovery topic at around 9.00 million euro expected contribution per project across an indicative three projects and a 26.50 million euro topic budget, with the parallel food topic at 3.00 million euro for one project.

## Activity window and actual dates

The deck shows Q3 2026 onwards. The topic opens on 13 October 2026 and closes on 2 February 2027.

## Scope as drafted

The learning and adaptation content in the horizontal part appears in three places. In the automated discovery topic, the requirements are for autonomous or semi-autonomous and adaptive AI systems including agentic AI connected to laboratory instruments and robotics that can plan, act, learn and adapt within a validated safe pipeline; scalable automation solutions and networked AI systems enabling collaborative experimentation across multiple laboratories and networks of laboratories, supporting simultaneous execution of large volumes of experiments; systems providing real-time data processing and analytics that enable immediate feedback and dynamic adjustments during experiments; standards and protocols ensuring interoperability between instruments, robotics and AI systems; and AI-driven predictive maintenance that optimises equipment uptime and resource utilisation.

In the Frontier AI scaling topic, the learning content is methodological: robust scaling laws and predictive frameworks, automated scaling-law derivation across modalities and tasks, exploratory architecture search including sparsity, memory efficiency and long-context handling, and dataset mixture optimisation including automated data quality assessment. In the bridging innovation topics, adaptive AI appears in the energy and mobility context — generative, agentic and embedded AI delivering secure cross-border flexibility and smart grid services, and level 4 autonomous driving in real urban traffic.

## Expected outcomes and impacts

The outcomes that a robot learning bid should claim are the automated discovery outcomes: closed-loop systems integrating automation with AI-driven trustworthy decision-making in existing environments; accelerated discovery with increased efficiency and reproducibility; improved productivity; advancement of laboratory automation including best practice and challenge identification; and prototype functional demonstrators. The learning-specific outcome is the working intelligence layer that allows instrumentation or a robot cell to plan, run and analyse tasks semi- or fully autonomously.

## Conditions, eligibility and TRL

- Automated discovery topic: Research and Innovation Action, around 9.00 million euro per project, three projects, 26.50 million euro budget, Chinese-entity participation barred, software development prioritised with complementary equipment permitted if justified.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration between computer and AI scientists and domain scientists is a stated expectation, not a preference.
- The detailed hardware targets must be described against a real laboratory or cell, since proposals must show how an existing facility is retrofitted without complete redesign.

## Fit for HWU / National Robotarium

Robot learning is a designated strength on both sides of the Edinburgh partnership. On the company side, Atomic Architect, Kelvin Nanotechnology, Neuranics, RoboK and Reath are the natural partners for instrumentation and adaptive perception.

## Points to watch

- No horizontal call topic matches this area as an autonomous robotics topic; the match is by subject overlap into the automated discovery topic, which dictates that the application must be scientific experimentation.
- Competing on the same topic from two Edinburgh institutions would be counterproductive given the small number of grants; a single combined bid with distinct work packages is the better play.
- Real-time data processing and interoperability standards are explicit requirements and should be resourced as deliverables rather than as supporting activity.
