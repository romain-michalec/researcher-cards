# 5 · Assistive Robotics & Independent Living

National Robotarium · Delivery Board · Horizon Europe 2026–2027

Area 5 of 16 in the opportunity pipeline · Detail drawn from the draft Horizon Europe Work Programme 2026-2027, part 14 Horizontal Activities

This document covers area 5 of the National Robotarium's 16 Horizon Europe 2026-2027 opportunity areas. There is no assistive robotics topic in Horizontal Activities; the same value band applies instead to small coordination instruments, and the nearest usable vehicle is set out below.

## Work Programme call and topic

The deck places this under Cluster 1 Health with a value of 500,000 to 1.5 million euro and an activity window from Q3 2026 onwards. That value and profile correspond to a Coordination and Support Action scale of activity rather than a large research action. Within Horizontal Activities, the closest instrument is HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-05-01, the Skills Bridging Action, which is a Coordination and Support Action, although its grants of 0.15 to 0.30 million euro per project are well below the deck's range. This section records the position and identifies where an assistive-robotics framing could attach.

## Indicative value

The deck values this area at 500,000 to 1.5 million euro, which is closer to the scale of the small coordination instruments in Horizontal Activities than to the research actions. The tasks in the Bridging actions call with contributions of around 1.50 million euro include HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-03-07, on research and technology infrastructures as strategic assets for talent, with 7.50 million euro across five projects.

## Activity window and actual dates

The deck shows Q3 2026 onwards. The nearest coordination- and skills-type calls open on 20 October 2026 with a deadline of 16 February 2027 (Skills Bridging Action) and on 9 March 2027 with a deadline of 15 June 2027 (research infrastructure access and talent).

## Scope as drafted

There is no assistive robotics or independent living topic in Horizontal Activities. Assistive robotics content in Horizontal Activities attaches to three places. First, the AI in Science automated discovery topic, where assistive and interactive safe AI-managed robotic systems that automate diverse experiments and can be applied to diverse hardware setups are an explicit research target; the assistive framing there is laboratory-facing rather than domestic. Second, the One Health topic's third group of activity, on tools and solutions that mainstream biodiversity in One Health, which touches health-supporting environments rather than home care. Third, health research infrastructure access, where biomedical technologies are the first target area for trans-national and virtual access.

The concrete scope of the health-adjacent access topic, HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-03-06, is worth setting out because it is the most usable small-scale health instrument in Horizontal Activities. It provides trans-national access, on-site or remote, and virtual access to integrated and customised research infrastructure services for industry-driven research, in six priority areas, the first of which is biomedical technologies. Access targets users from industry, notably SMEs, startups and scaleups, or from academia when collaborating with industry. Proposals should include an outreach and engagement plan, should include at least one ESFRI Landmark or European Research Infrastructure Consortium as beneficiary, and should provide a portfolio of complementary services of European interest including data services and customised workflows. Data management in line with FAIR principles and connection of digital services to the European Open Science Cloud should be addressed.

## Expected outcomes and impacts

For health research infrastructure access: research infrastructures improve their attractiveness for industry so they can sustainably extend their user base, with future continuity with technology infrastructures facilitated where appropriate; researchers and innovators get more efficient access to the best research infrastructures irrespective of location, through a single-entry-point access portal and converging access conditions; breakthrough research is enabled by advanced services made available to a wider user community; a new generation of researchers and innovators is trained to exploit the tools for industry-driven research, with attention to early-career researchers and those from widening countries; and industry awareness of relevant facilities and services is raised.

## Conditions, eligibility and TRL

- HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-03-06: Research and Innovation Action, total indicative budget 15.00 million euro, expected contribution around 5.00 million euro per project, three projects expected, application page limit 100 pages.
- Access provision activities must be included in the proposal; applicants should read the Specific features for Research Infrastructures section of the work programme part before preparing the application.
- Award criteria are extended so that under Excellence the extent of access activities offering state-of-the-art infrastructures and high-quality services is assessed, as is the contribution to facilitating and integrating access procedures and further developing on-line services.
- Procedure: to ensure a balanced portfolio, grants are awarded to the highest-ranked proposals within each area, provided thresholds are met.
- Beneficiaries are subject to additional access rights: they must grant royalty-free access to intellectual property relating to tools, standards, specifications and other relevant outputs directly relevant to advertising the infrastructure services and their modality of access and to implementing access calls, to EU institutions and to beneficiaries of EU-funded projects, during and beyond the life of the grant agreement. Eligible costs may take the form of unit costs for trans-national and virtual access to research infrastructures.
- The Joint Research Centre may participate with zero funding or as an associated partner.

## Fit for HWU / National Robotarium

Assistive and independent-living robotics is an area where Heriot-Watt has genuine depth.

Because the horizontal part lacks a matching topic, the practical options for this area are the mainstream Cluster 1 health work programme, or an assistive-robotics framing inside the AI in Science automated discovery topic if the project can be framed around assistive robotic systems as automation of experimental or care procedures, or a partnership route through the health research infrastructure access topic for shared facilities. The Delivery Board should record that this area, like area 4, is currently without a matched horizontal call.

## Points to watch

- No horizontal call topic matches this area; the value band in the deck is not evidenced in the source.
- The access topic's requirements are procedural and rights-based rather than research-based: the bidding effort sits in access provision, governance and outreach, not in robotics development.
- An ESFRI Landmark or ERIC must be in the consortium for the access topic, which usually means partnering rather than leading.
