# 6 · Human-Robot Interaction & Trustworthy AI

National Robotarium · Delivery Board · Horizon Europe 2026–2027

Area 6 of 16 in the opportunity pipeline · Detail drawn from the draft Horizon Europe Work Programme 2026-2027, part 14 Horizontal Activities

This document covers area 6 of the National Robotarium's 16 Horizon Europe 2026-2027 opportunity areas. There is no Cluster 4 topic in Horizontal Activities; the trustworthiness requirements appear inside the AI topics, and the closest template is set out below.

## Work Programme call and topic

The deck places this under Cluster 4 Digital with an activity window from summer 2026 and a value of 5-10 million euro. Horizontal Activities contains no Cluster 4 topic. The horizontal instruments that carry HRI and trustworthy AI content are the AI in Science call, whose automated discovery topic requires human oversight and interaction to ensure accuracy, safety and ethical compliance, and the EU AI Media Platform topic in the Bridging actions call, which requires transparent and accountable AI practices, including documentation, explainability, logging and evaluation.

## Indicative value

The deck values this area at 5-10 million euro. The horizontal topics with the nearest value are HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-02-02 at 2.50 to 4.00 million euro per project and HORIZON-RAISE-2027-01-01 at around 9.00 million euro per project. The EU AI Media Platform topic, at 10.00 million euro for one project, is the closest single value in the horizontal part, but its subject is media rather than robotics.

## Activity window and actual dates

The deck shows summer 2026 onwards. The relevant horizontal deadlines are 2 February 2027 for AI in Science automated discovery, 18 March 2027 for the Apply AI topics, and 28 January 2027 for the valorisation and innovation topics.

## Scope as drafted

The trustworthy AI content in the horizontal part is strongest in the EU AI Media Platform topic, HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-01-09, which is a useful reference for how the Commission expects trustworthy AI to be evidenced even though the subject matter differs. That topic requires built-in safety, transparency and governance features; fair human-data interactions such as legibility, agency and negotiability; traceability of key decisions; open evaluation methods; validated governance and interoperability frameworks; reliable tracking of content origins through provenance information, attribution metadata and verification signals; and AI components that follow transparent and accountable practices including documentation, explainability, logging and evaluation, to enable audit, redress and effective oversight. Human-machine interaction content also appears in the automated discovery topic through the requirement for intuitive user interfaces and for human oversight and interaction in closed-loop systems.

## Expected outcomes and impacts

For the media platform topic, the outcomes of interest as a template are reliable, modular and interoperable infrastructure with validated service levels and sustainability pathways; content in multiple languages with equal access for all types of organisation; built-in safety, transparency and governance features with fair human-data interactions and traceable decisions; and reliable tracking of content origins. The transferable principle for an HRI bid is that trustworthiness is evidenced through mechanisms — provenance, logging, oversight, redress — rather than asserted as a design value.

## Conditions, eligibility and TRL

- EU AI Media Platform: Innovation Action, TRL 6 at start to TRL 8 by the end, 10.00 million euro for one project, subject to restrictions for the protection of European communication networks.
- AI in Science automated discovery: Research and Innovation Action, around 9.00 million euro per project, three projects across a 26.50 million euro budget, Chinese-entity participation barred.
- Apply AI topics generally: Chinese-entity participation barred across both Research and Innovation Actions and Innovation Actions in the Bridging actions call.

## Fit for HWU / National Robotarium

Human-robot interaction and trustworthy AI are among Heriot-Watt's strongest and most visible research areas, and together with the University of Edinburgh they form a genuinely strong combined group on interaction and assurance.

Because there is no matching horizontal topic, the practical route is either the mainstream Cluster 4 digital work programme or a framing inside the automated discovery topic, where human oversight and interaction in autonomous systems is a stated requirement rather than a bolt-on. A trustworthy-AI framing inside the Apply AI topics is possible but a weaker subject fit. The Delivery Board should record that this area is currently without a matched horizontal call.

## Points to watch

- No horizontal call topic matches this area; the value band is not evidenced in the source.
- If the automated discovery topic is used as the vehicle, the proposal must lead on the closed-loop experimentation system, with trustworthiness as a requirement inside it rather than as the subject.
- The Chinese-entity eligibility bar applies to every AI topic in the horizontal part and will need to be checked against any proposed partner list.
