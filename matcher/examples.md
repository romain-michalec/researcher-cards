# Example questions for the matcher

Questions to ask the Claude Project, followed by checks with known answers for testing the Project after the cards or area files change.

## Quick start

Six questions that show most of what the Project can do.

1. **Start from an opportunity:** "Which academics are the best fit for opportunity 15 (offshore and marine: inspection, maintenance and repair)? Explain why for each."
2. **Start from a person:** "Which of the 16 opportunities best suit Marta Vallejo?"
3. **Plan a bid:** "Suggest a lead and two supporting academics for opportunities 4 (healthcare and surgical robotics) and 5 (assistive robotics and independent living), as a table I can paste into a slide."
4. **Check deadlines:** "Which opportunity areas close first?"
5. **Find the gaps:** "Which profiles are too thin to judge? I'll contact those people directly."
6. **Look beyond the files:** "Could you search for companies in Scotland working on underwater inspection robotics that might partner on opportunity 15?"

## More questions to try

### Starting from an opportunity area

- "Who could support area 5 (assistive robotics and independent living), even if they aren't an obvious lead?"
- "Who should lead a bid for opportunity 4 (healthcare and surgical robotics), and who should support them?"

### Starting from a subject

- "Who should lead a bid on underwater robotics, and who could support them?"

### Starting from a person

- "Which of the 16 opportunities best suit Christian Dondrup?" His card is thin, so Claude should say that little is known rather than rule him out.
- "What does Marta Romeo's profile say she works on? Quote the relevant parts."

### Deadlines, budgets and conditions

- "How much could a single project get under area 3, and how does that compare with the Delivery Board figure?"
- "Can Heriot-Watt apply directly under area 11?"
- "Which official calls and topics does area 15 point to?"

### Planning bids

- "Which opportunities have no strong candidate at all?"
- "Which academics appear as candidates for several opportunities? Could any of them work together on a joint bid?"

### Thin profiles and looking further

- "Valentina Lo Gatto's profile is thin. What would help to judge her fit for the opportunities?" Claude should suggest a specific search and wait for a yes before doing it.
- "Yes, look up her recent publications." Claude should then keep what it finds separate from the card, and say where each piece came from.

### Partners

- "Which companies could partner on area 15?"
- "Do any of the academics already have links with companies?"

### Contacting people

- "Draft a short email inviting Yvan Petillot to discuss opportunity 15, saying why he fits."
